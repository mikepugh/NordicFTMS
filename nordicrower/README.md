# NordicRower

**Bluetooth rowing measurements for compatible NordicTrack/ProForm rowers.**

NordicRower runs on Wolf/iFIT USB rower consoles and shares power, stroke rate
and stroke count over Bluetooth FTMS. Connect from your fitness app on another
device and keep NordicRower in the foreground during your workout.

[Download](https://github.com/mikepugh/NordicFTMS/releases/tag/nordicrower-v1.0.1) ·
[Installation](docs/install.md) · [Guide en français](docs/install-fr.md)

## Features

- Live power, stroke rate and stroke count.
- Clear USB and Bluetooth connection status.
- Guided recovery if the USB connection is busy.
- Diagnostic reports you can save or share.

NordicRower provides measurements only. Remote resistance, ERG, speed, distance
and pace are not supported. Apps requiring an FTMS Control Point are unsupported.

For compatible GlassOS consoles, use [NordicFTMS](../README.md).

Contributor information is in the [development guide](docs/development.md).
